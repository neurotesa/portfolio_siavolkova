AIn8nAutomation

Akiva

A marketing engine for an EdTech startup, built solo on n8n and AI agents.

Role

Everything, solo: research, automation, content, ads, branding.

Project

Pet project for Akiva Immersive Learning, a VR learning system for neurodivergent kids.

The starting point

35 followers, no funnel, no system.

Akiva had scattered docs, no brand identity, no content engine and no outreach funnel. Normally this takes an SMM specialist, a designer and an ad manager. Here it was one person with AI workflows and tools.

Results

14× growth in one week of ads.

  • 18,380views, +38,192%
  • 441new followers, +44,000%
  • $0.13cost per click vs $1–2 industry average
  • 70%less manual work through automation

One post hit Facebook trends on August 31. Overall, the setup cost 3–4× less than a typical agency while working like an in-house team.

The system

Five parts, one pipeline.

  1. Research & SWOTCompetitors and audience pains.
  2. AI workflowAutomated text generation with a consistent tone of voice.
  3. Video contentAI avatars, auto-subtitles, A/B testing.
  4. FunnelLinktree and a free VR demo app.
  5. Brand identityLogo, mini brandbook, website.

How it works

Content topics

An n8n workflow scrapes Reddit, keeps posts with 500+ upvotes, checks relevance and extracts the pains parents actually talk about. Those pains become hooks and post ideas.

Text content

AI agents trained on Akiva's docs write in the brand's voice: hooks, posts, lifehacks and video scripts, all saved to Google Sheets.

Video and lead magnet

Scripts go to VEED for AI-avatar videos with stock footage, subtitles and music, and to Canva for bulk carousels. From Meta, comment auto-replies lead to Linktree and an n8n form. Brevo sends the lead magnet, the contact goes to Pipedrive, and a personalized email campaign follows.

Branding

One look across every channel.

I designed the logo and a mini brandbook, rebuilt the website with clearer messaging and unified the visuals across all socials.
