UULA

3D illustrations, animations and video for Kuwait's largest EdTech platform and its Saudi version.

Role

UI Designer / 3D & Product Visual. All 3D and motion solo, working with the art director.

Company

UULA Technologies, 2022–2025

Markets

Kuwait, Saudi Arabia. Arabic interface, RTL.

Tools

Blender, Midjourney, After Effects, Lottie, Figma

The task

Studying versus games and social media.

UULA is an online learning platform for school students: video lessons, notes, tests and questions to teachers, following Kuwait's curriculum. The Saudi version also prepares students for the Qudurat and Tahsili exams.

For these students, studying is a boring obligation, and the product competes for their attention with games and social media. My job was to make it beautiful and fun for kids: build a recognisable 3D style and fill the website and app with it. Assets had to ship fast, since new courses, bundles and seasonal campaigns kept coming.

Style

Plastic toys and building blocks.

Bright, saturated colours, soft rounded shapes and tactile materials: glossy and matte plastic. Soft studio light with highlights. One hero object on a platform or an “island”, with small details around it.

The core of the style is a set of 3D objects modelled in Blender, one per subject. A phone booth for English, a molecule for chemistry, DNA for biology, an arch for Arabic. The objects travel across the whole product: subject covers, cases, stories.

The same illustration shipped in different colourways to fit each page.

Feature stories

Instagram stories for UULA that explain app features. In each one, the feature's 3D object flies into a real app screen. The Zubda story plays on the literal translation: in Arabic the word means both “the essence of a subject” and “butter”.

AI in production

30 illustrations in a week instead of a month.

We had to ship a feature with 30 illustrations very fast, and there was no time to make them in 3D. I trained Midjourney on our style and made all the illustrations with it. At the time, the team was only starting to adopt Midjourney.

  1. StyleTrained Midjourney on the UULA style.
  2. 2 daysMade 30 illustrations.
  3. 2 daysApproval.
  4. 1 dayIllustrations added to the product.

The approach reconstructed on new objects: one style, dozens of scenes.
