CryptoiOS

Bitlime

A crypto exchange that doesn't make you feel like a trader.

Role

Product designer, end to end: research, structure, wireframes, UI, design system.

Scope

iOS app, 50 screens.

Hero image

The task

Design a mobile app where people can buy, sell and track crypto, for everyone from first-time investors to active traders.

The problem

Big exchanges are built for people who already trade.

A beginner opens the app and sees too many buttons, words like P2P, Spot or TRC20, and a home screen that shows everything at once. Even the first deposit takes about 10 steps, and it's easy to get lost on the way.

Research

Who it's for

I described five types of users: investor, trader, beginner, business owner and student. They all want speed, safety and low fees. Beginners also want guidance, traders want more tools.

Personas

What users told me

I talked to two people who already use crypto exchanges:

  • They liked simple interfaces and learning materials.
  • They didn't like high fees and complicated apps.
  • They chose a new app based on ease of use, fees and security.
  • Before switching, they worried about fees and about how support works.

Competitors

I compared Binance, Bybit and Bitget screen by screen. They are built almost the same way. The main difference is how much they show at once. None of them asks early whether you're a beginner or not.

Competitor analysis

User journey

I walked through the path from opening the app to the first deposit and noted every question and problem. The hardest step was choosing a network: you only see names, with no minimum amount, no arrival time and no easy way back.

Customer journey map

Structure

The app has five main sections: Home, Markets, Trade, Futures and Assets, plus a Profile. The deposit is split into small, simple steps instead of one crowded screen.

App map

Before working on visuals, I built a clickable wireframe prototype to test the flows.

Solution

First entry

Before signing up, the user sees what their account will look like: a locked balance with "1,000 USDT will be here", one Registration button and an option to try demo trading. They can also pick favorite pairs right away.

Registration and verification

Short forms, clear error messages and a simple checklist of what you need for verification.

Home

The main action, Buy / Deposit / P2P, is the biggest button on the screen, because in research users couldn't find it.

Deposit in four steps

Each step answers a question users had:

  1. Choose a methodEach option has a short explanation.
  2. Pick a coinPopular coins and search.
  3. Pick a networkConfirmations, minimum amount and arrival time for each.
  4. Send to the addressQR code, copy and share.

Trade

A simple buy/sell form, an order book and stop-loss / take-profit settings. Every state is designed: empty, typing, filled, sell and open order.

Markets

A list of pairs with filters, a pair page with a chart, and analytics on money flow.

Assets

One card with the total balance, a chart and asset split, buttons for Deposit, Send and Transfer, and a hide-balance mode.

Profile

Account info and VIP progress in one card. Risky actions like Disable account are moved to Settings.

Conversion

Swap one coin for another in one screen, with a clear error, a confirmation step and a success screen.

Visual style

Dark navy background and one bright lime color for the brand and main buttons. Price up and price down have their own colors, so the brand color never mixes with profit and loss. One font, Manrope, and one set of icons. Clear spacing: 32 between blocks, 12 inside a block. The name Bitlime comes from bit + lime, so the name and the colors work together.

How to measure success

The app hasn't launched yet, so these are the numbers I designed for:

  • ActivationHow many users make a first deposit after signing up.
  • Time to first actionHow fast people go from opening the app to their first deposit or trade.
  • Drop-offWhere people leave the deposit flow, especially at network selection.
  • Sign-up rateWhether the locked-balance screen brings more registrations than a regular promo banner.

Beyond the brief

All screen states are designed: errors, keyboard, empty states, confirmations, success and hidden balance. Interface texts are rewritten in plain English. The data is consistent across screens: one BTC price, balances that add up, conversions that match the rate.
